Hey, I'm Sreeram

25 · Tech background · Business at heart

The Story So Far

I'm 25 and somewhere in that exciting, slightly chaotic middle ground — old enough to know what I want, young enough to still be figuring out how to get there. I started out in tech, writing code, building products, learning how systems work from the ground up. And while I genuinely enjoyed it, I kept noticing that what really lit me up wasn't just the engineering — it was the why behind it. The business problem. The user. The strategy.

Turns out, I've had a quiet obsession with business for as long as I can remember — how companies grow, why products succeed or fail, what makes a great pitch, how decisions get made at the top. I just happened to take the longer route and come at it through code. Now I'm intentionally steering toward product and client-facing roles, where I can use my technical foundation as a superpower rather than a job title.

The Pivot

Moving from a coding role to product or business isn't a departure — it's an evolution. I want to be in rooms where strategy is made, where client relationships are built, where a deep understanding of technology lets you ask better questions than everyone else. My goal is to tap into the business-minded side of me that's always been there, armed with the rare advantage of actually knowing how the product is built.

Whether that's product management, solutions consulting, or client strategy in a tech-forward company — I'm actively navigating that path, one conversation and one opportunity at a time.

Off the Clock

Football is religion. I live and breathe the game — the tactics, the drama, the last-minute winners, the transfer window chaos. It's one of the few things that can make me genuinely emotional at 11pm on a Tuesday.

Formula 1 is the other obsession. I love it for all the reasons most people find it overwhelming — the engineering, the team strategy, the politics behind the pit wall. Every race weekend feels like a ten-episode thriller compressed into two hours.

When I'm not watching sport, I'm usually down a rabbit hole — tech news, startup stories, market shifts, what's happening in the world. I genuinely enjoy staying plugged in, forming opinions, and talking about ideas. Curiosity is probably my default state.
